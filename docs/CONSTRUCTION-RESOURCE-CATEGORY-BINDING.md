Preserve original assessment/category catalog identities. Reuse already bounded, immutable, version-checked imports; no new source reads, unbounded scans or client-provided source trust.

Binding gates describe source/category validity only, **not operation eligibility or numerical readiness**; assessment gates and later arithmetic/phase requirements remain mandatory.
