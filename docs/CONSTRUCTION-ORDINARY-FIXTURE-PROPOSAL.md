- Starting city/generator/mining/farming footprints count for placement and district limits; their initial economic contributions are **not applied**. Set initial laboratory capacities to **600 technician / 400 miner / 800 farmer**, housing to **0**, and initial infrastructure upkeep to **0** as explicit test baselines. - `building_capital`, `building_precinct_house`, `building_research_lab_1`, `building_factory_1` and `zone_default` are installed occupancy/display objects only: **all their starting jobs, housing, amenities, stability/crime, production, upkeep and other numerical effects are disabled** in this checkpoint. Their construction, replacement, removal and upgrade are unavailable. UI must label them as inactive laboratory footprints. Do not advertise a functioning capital/factory/research economy.
- Keep the selected authority/ethics/civics/trait identifiers for source predicates, but disable their numerical effects **except** the two Functional Architecture construction modifiers above. In particular, Mining Guilds, Ingenious bonus Workforce, authoritarian worker-output effects, Imperial capital-system production, materialist research/upkeep and Prosperous Unification's startup prosperity modifier are inactive. Other species habitability/living-standard/population effects and council hooks are inactive. Explicitly fixed bonus Workforce stays zero until the player's existing laboratory control changes it; no automatic trait application is claimed.
- No native startup resource/population generator, growth, auto-employment, happiness/stability/housing penalties, trade/logistics/policy conversion, deficit penalties, diplomacy, ruler lifecycle or research progression runs in this checkpoint. Existing M2 laboratory job arithmetic and manual assignment remain active; source-derived capacities bound ordinary assignment, not effective bonus Workforce. This is a significant temporary fidelity reduction, not a correction to the accepted content.
- Decrepit clearance is excluded under `decrepit-population-effect-unavailable`: its source `on_cleared` performs weighted species selection and `create_pop_group` (`common/deposits/01_blocker_deposits.txt:980–1012`). Do not execute a penalty-only approximation, invent population quantity, or substitute Failing behavior. Tradeoff: the checkpoint tests two real named Failing clearances but cannot clear every displayed blocker.
- Finished-object demolition, destruction, colonization/abandonment, ownership transfer and mobile-colony transitions are inaccessible through gameplay in this checkpoint. Ownership-change cancellation may be tested through the existing transaction harness under C03, but is not an enabled conquest feature. Static/paused colonies make no progress or settlement; no destruction refund rule is inferred. Fleet movement cannot destroy or capture a colony. No combat is introduced.
- Request deferral of **hive and machine construction**, **Voidfarers mobile construction and carrier lifecycle**, the scientific/military ordinary role setups, and other accepted construction entries **from this checkpoint only**. None are reclassified as rejected content. If the human does not approve this scope amendment, this fixture is only a component test and cannot close the required combined stage.
