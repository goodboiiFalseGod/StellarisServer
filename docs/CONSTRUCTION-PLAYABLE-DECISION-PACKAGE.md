## One complete resolution, not an approval waterfall

Before requesting a single human approval, the integrator/orchestrator must include the realized fixture(s) and exact supported operations above alongside the following disposition for **every** residual item:

1. C01–C08: either source/native evidence closes each rule, or approve the exact named/versioned prototype rule already proposed. No blanket permission to guess other rules.
2. Startup: choose source-backed realized class/size/flags/layout/occupancy/deposit instances for each required axis. Any extra vacant zone, simplified startup, fixed underlying blocker pairing or omitted startup numerical effect must be an enumerated departure, not an unnamed “test fixture”. Current source-backed candidates are listed above; still missing choices are not replaced by numbers.
3. Clearance: complete Failing Infrastructure's actual pairing/restoration and Decrepit's population effects, or explicitly approve a narrowly named exclusion/departure. There is currently no numerical population proposal to approve. Source/native research remains the first route; the human is not asked to invent a vanilla formula.
4. Cancellation/lifecycle: C03 covers player/condition-loss/ownership refund, **not destruction**. Destruction credit policy and complete ownership/static/mobile transitions must be sourced/approved or explicitly inaccessible in this bounded delivery. No silent zero refund. Completed-object demolition is excluded by proposed C07, not confused with cancellation.
5. Numerical effects: per operation, list included modifiers/jobs/capacity/housing and unsupported reachable effects. Preserve D38/D43 and accepted source content. An unsupported mandatory effect prevents that operation until resolved; unused unrelated content need not block it.
6. Delivery claim: either retain all required axes and finish their configurations/operations, or ask for an explicit stage-boundary amendment naming exactly what is deferred. An ordinary-only working loop may be a useful checkpoint but cannot be advertised as full accepted-stage completion without that amendment.

Engineering coordination is now implemented in the trusted aggregate: shared balance reconciliation, checked candidate debit/refund, multi-colony completion/single settlement and immutable exact-retry publication. Native/startup decisions are not hidden inside it. Outer session must still publish calendar/spatial/participant state with the aggregate under its lock. Final acceptance includes two players/observer, local images, successful district/zone/building/clearance/workforce result, negative authority/resource/slot/stale/retry cases, cancel/ownership/overflow/day-month rollback and combined browser checks; disclose each excluded or unverified condition before manual handoff.
